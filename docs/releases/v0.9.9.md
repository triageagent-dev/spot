- **A reply answers the request Tria is waiting for.** The model chooses the `reply_to` of a reply in the bus, and left alone it answered a request it had already answered, again, while Tria's newer request waited, or threaded its reply to one of its own messages: Tria then carried an old answer to the person as the answer to the new question, or took the reply for a report nobody asked for and spoke in the team room, and the new request was never worked on. The supervisor now keeps a book of the bus from every page it polls and every page the model reads: Tria's requests, the spot's own messages and its replies by the request they answer. While a request of Tria's is open (unanswered, due, below the hop limit), a reply must answer one of her requests: a reply to the spot's own message or to a message the book does not know as hers, and a second reply to a request she already has the answer to while a newer one waits, are refused as a tool result that names the open request, quotes it and says to answer that one (or, for new word on an old request, to post it with no `reply_to`). A reply to an unanswered request of hers goes, and so does any reply while nothing of hers is open, so a partial answer can still be completed.
