A spot is an A2A agent of its workspace and says so with an agent card (the core's ADR-114).

- **Agent card on hello.** Every hello, on the join socket and on the link, carries `card`: an A2A `AgentCard` with the spot's name, what it is for, its version, the extension `https://triageagent.dev/a2a/spot/v1` and its skills. The core keeps it on the spot's record and shows it in `list_spots`; Tria picks a spot by its card when the workspace has several, naming the spot or a skill.
- **Skills.** `--description` says what the spot is for; `--skills-file` or `--skills-json` (`[{id, name, description, tags}]`, 32 at most) adds skills of the install's own. The built-in `cluster` skill and one skill per connector (`connector:<name>`, its tools as tags, its hint as the description) come by themselves; a given skill replaces a built-in of the same id. Chart values `spot.description` and `spot.skills`.
