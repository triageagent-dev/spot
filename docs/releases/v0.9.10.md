A timer wake that has nothing to do costs no model call, and a refused token on connect is retried.

- **A timer wake after a turn that did nothing makes no model call.** A model that found nothing still planned its next wake, and the supervisor counted every such wake as asked for by the agent, so it ran a turn each time: the whole prompt and tool list, twice, to read an empty room. On an actor it was worse: the core sends the words of the plan with its order and not who made it, so every timer wake looked asked for, the supervisor's own quiet plan included, and the quiet rule never applied. The supervisor now keeps in its state directory who made the plan and whether the last timer wake's turn only read the room and planned the next wake. A wake that follows such a turn, or a plan the supervisor made itself, is not counted as asked for; with nothing to show it ends without a model call, with an issue open too, until something changes or 12 hours have passed. A turn that does any other work, a message and an event are as before.
- **A wake whose facts are no longer true makes no model call.** A wake carried by changes to an issue that closed before the wake ran had its facts dropped and its edges kept, and ran a turn with nothing to show. It is now quiet.
- **A core that refuses the token on connect gets one fresh hello and one more try.** An actor's first connect of a wake could meet "authorization required" (the core revokes a token issued before it marked the actor suspended). The turn failed, the wake was lost, and the retry replaced a long plan with a wake two minutes later.
- The hourly token ceiling is its own pause reason, with the time the spot expects to wake; a connector with `auth: injected` sends a placeholder bearer for an actor's egress to replace.
